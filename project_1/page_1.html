Get to know me!

Hey! My name is Julia Barbosa Solorzano! I am currently a first year student at Illinois Tech! I am studying Game Design and Experiemntal media, and I'd like to focus in UX Design in the future!

I was born and raised in a neighborhood around Mexico City! I moved to Rexas for High School, where I attended the School for the Talented and Gifted. Then, I moved to Chicago to attend Illinois Tech as an international student.

In my journey in college, I've been exporing the city, meeting people, embracing new opportunities and responsibilities and transitioning into adulthood.

I have also joined many clubs in my college, such as SHPE, IGDA, the Radio Station, and the newspaper.

In my free time, I enjoy drawing, hanging out with my friends, listening to music, and working out.

I am very passionate and hardworking, and I am excited to see what other opportunities I am presented with during this journey!
